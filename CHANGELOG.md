# Changelog

## 0.9.0 (beta)

First public beta.

- Live sim wind, temperature and ISA temperature, with a compass and headwind and crosswind components
- Forecast winds in 1,000 ft steps around your aircraft, from GFS or ECMWF IFS
- A record of the winds the sim reported at each level you pass through
- Your latest SimBrief plan compared with fresh forecast winds, including surface winds at the departure and destination airports
- A Sim est. column estimating the sim's winds along the route
- Winds for any custom point and altitude
- A recommended cruise level from FL290 to FL510, with an estimate of the fuel difference from your plan
- Runs in its own window, with no installation
